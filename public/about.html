🚌 About This Project

A live GPS bus tracker built for DHSGSU students, to solve real waiting-time uncertainty with only 2 campus buses in service.

How it works

The driver (and conductor) share live GPS from their phone's browser. Students see the bus move on a map in real time, with speed, distance, and ETA.

Having an issue?

Use the "Report an Issue" button on the main map, or reach out directly.
